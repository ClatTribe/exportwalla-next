    Your worries, answered

    Exporting feels risky. Here is how we keep it safe.

    Will I get paid?

    Never ship on open credit to a new buyer

    We set up advance, letter of credit or ECGC-covered terms, and check the LC documents before you ship.

    Are the buyers real?

    Only buyers with a record

    UK importers come from HMRC's own import records for your HS code. Elsewhere our desk verifies shipment history; we never show guessed names.

    So much paperwork?

    We prepare, you sign

    Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, shipping bill checks and refunds are drafted for you. Licensed signatures stay with you and your CA.

    What will it cost?

    Free to check, pay per shipment

    HS code, markets, duty, buyers and quotes are free. The desk charges per shipment, with no subscription and no share of your order.

    Is my English enough?

    Hindi or English, your choice

    Talk to the desk in Hindi or English. Emails to buyers are drafted for you to approve.

    Do I lose control?

    You stay the exporter of record

    Your IEC, your buyer, your GST refund. We never take title to your goods or sit between you and your customer.
